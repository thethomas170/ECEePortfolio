Welcome

Where I am and where I am headed

Thanks for visiting. This site is my ePortfolio: a running record of the projects I build, the coursework I complete, the experience I pick up, and the plan I am following to get from a first-year ECE student to a working engineer in the electric energy industry. I started it in ECE 1100 during my first semester at Georgia Tech, and I intend to keep adding to it every semester until I graduate.

Right now my focus is narrowing in on electric energy systems and circuit technology. Electricity demand is climbing faster than the infrastructure that delivers it, and the people who understand how to design, build, and manage that infrastructure are going to stay busy for a long time. That is the problem I want to work on, and the pages below show what I am doing about it.
